# Zalim Marketing Page Inventory

## Reference-inspired public structure

The Zalim Marketing experience will use an original interpretation of the following public marketplace patterns observed on the reference storefront: a delivery notice, vehicle selector, popular-category discovery, high-selling product area, vehicle-accessory category links, product catalog, cart, checkout, customer account, and mailing-list entry point.

## Original implementation rules

Zalim Marketing will not use the reference site’s code, brand, logos, product names, images, external links, text, customer reviews, ratings, or testimonials. The new site will use its own visual system, fictional but clearly labeled catalog content until the owner adds real inventory, and an empty customer-review state rather than fabricated social proof.

## Planned Zalim Marketing route coverage

| Area | Route family | Original Zalim Marketing treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `/` | Vehicle-led discovery, essential categories, offers, new additions, and a non-review service promise. |
| Vehicle discovery | `/vehicle-finder` | Make, model, and year selection that filters compatible catalog items. |
| Catalog | `/shop`, `/collections/*`, `/brands/*`, `/search` | Category-driven browsing, product filters, search, sort, pagination, and list/grid options. |
| Product | `/products/*` | Product media, compatibility details, specifications, stock, cart action, and delivery details. |
| Commerce | `/cart`, `/checkout`, `/order-confirmation` | Cart, address selection, secure-payment readiness, cancellation recovery, and order status. |
| Customer | `/account`, `/account/orders/*` | Orders, saved addresses, account details, and authentication entry points. |
| Support | `/about`, `/contact`, `/faq`, `/shipping`, `/returns` | Owner-led store information without public phone or email placeholders. |
| Administration | `/admin` | Role-gated catalog, order, category, and brand controls. |
